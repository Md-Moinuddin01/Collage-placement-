About GGITS Placement Portal

A student-made demo portal for campus placement management.

About the College

Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology & Science, Jabalpur is known for technical education, training activities and campus placement support. This demo website shows how a simple digital portal can connect students, companies and the placement cell.

The project is made using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Flask and SQLite for portfolio purpose only.

For Students

Track applications and improve skills.

For Companies

View applicants and hiring status.

For College

Manage drives, reports and placement data.
